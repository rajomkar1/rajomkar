Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Hikaro ("we", "us"), a sole proprietorship registered in India, collects and uses personal data when you use our websites and products.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, country and billing status.
  • Instagram data (with your permission): your Instagram professional account ID and username, an access token, and the comments and messages on your account needed to run the automations you set up.
  • Data about people who interact with your account: their Instagram-scoped ID, username, the comment or message they sent, and any email or phone number they choose to share in a conversation.
  • Payment data: handled by our payment providers (Razorpay, Paddle). We never see or store full card or UPI details.
  • Technical data: basic logs (time, request type, errors) kept for security, without personal content.

How we use data

Only to provide the service you asked for: running your automations, showing your dashboard, billing, support, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share data only with providers needed to run the service: Meta (Instagram API), Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Razorpay and Paddle (payments), and email providers for service messages. Each processes data under its own privacy terms.

Retention

Automation logs are deleted after 30 days. Account and contact data are kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account or disconnect Instagram, unless the law requires us to keep billing records longer.

Security

Access tokens are encrypted, connections use HTTPS, every account's data is isolated, and access to systems is restricted and logged.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data at any time, and withdraw consent by disconnecting Instagram. Users in India have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; users in the UK and EU have rights under the GDPR. See Data Deletion or email us.

Children

Our services are not intended for anyone under 18.
