Data Deletion Instructions

You can delete your data at any time in any of these ways:

  1. In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your data is removed within 30 days.
  2. Disconnect Instagram: Instagram app → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove our app. We receive Meta's deletion request and delete the related data within 30 days.
  3. By email: write to hello@hikarohq.com from your account email with the subject "Delete my data".

If someone messaged an account that uses our service and wants their data removed, they can email us with their Instagram username and the account they contacted.

Billing records may be kept longer where the law requires.
